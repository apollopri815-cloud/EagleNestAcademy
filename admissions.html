Application Process

How to Apply in 5 Simple Steps

We have made the EagleNest application process as straightforward as possible. From downloading the form to receiving your offer letter, the entire journey typically takes [REPLACE: 2–3 weeks].

Applications for January 2026 intake are now open. We recommend applying early — places at each level are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-assessed basis.

Download the Application Form

Print, complete, and return the form to our offices or scan and email to admissions@eaglenest.ac.ke . The form is also available at the school reception.

Download Application Form (PDF)
  1. 1

    Download & Complete the Application Form

    Download the application form from our website or collect a physical copy from the school reception. Fill in all sections clearly in block letters. The form is free of charge.

  2. 2

    Submit Your Documents

    Return the completed form together with all required supporting documents (see checklist below) to our admissions office or email admissions@eaglenest.ac.ke .

  3. 3

    Learner Assessment & Campus Tour

    We invite your child for a friendly, low-pressure assessment session. This is not a competitive exam — it helps us place your child in the right class for their level. Parents are welcome to tour the campus on the same day.

  4. 4

    Receive Your Offer Letter

    Successful applicants will receive an official Offer of Admission via email and post within [REPLACE: 7–10 school days] of the assessment. The letter confirms the class, intake date, and fee payment instructions.

  5. 5

    Pay Fees & Confirm Enrolment

    Pay the registration and term fees as instructed in your offer letter to confirm your child's place. You will then receive the uniform list, stationery requirements, and a welcome pack. Congratulations — your child is now part of the EagleNest family! 🎉
