The EagleNest Difference

Why Families Choose Us

We combine academic rigour with holistic development — because great education is about more than grades.

CBC-Aligned Curriculum

Fully aligned to the KICD Competency Based Curriculum, delivered by TSC-registered teachers trained in CBC pedagogy and learner-centred approaches.

Small Class Sizes

A maximum of 30 learners per class ensures every child receives individual attention. Our teachers know each learner by name — and by strength.

Safe & Nurturing Environment

Our gated, CCTV-monitored campus and trained pastoral care team ensure every child feels safe, valued, and emotionally supported every single day.

Co-Curricular Excellence

Over 20 clubs and sports — football, volleyball, swimming, debate, choir, drama, and more — discover and develop the hidden talent in every learner.

Modern Facilities

Science labs, a digital library, ICT suites with internet connectivity, a full-size sports field, and a purpose-built art studio — everything a 21st-century learner needs.

Strong Parent Partnership

Regular progress reports, parent-teacher forums, and a dedicated parent portal keep families informed and actively involved in their child's learning journey.

Admissions 2026

Joining EagleNest is Simple

Applications for January 2026 intake are now open. Follow these four straightforward steps to secure your child's place.

  1. 1

    Download the Form

    Download the Application Form from our website or collect it from the school office. It's free of charge.

  2. 2

    Submit Documents

    Return the completed form with certified birth certificate, passport photos, and most recent school report (if applicable).

  3. 3

    Assessment Day

    Attend our friendly learner assessment and campus tour. This helps us place your child in the right class for their individual level.

  4. 4

    Confirm Enrolment

    Receive your offer letter, pay the required fees, and collect your child's uniform list. Welcome to the EagleNest family!
