Curriculum Framework

What Is the Competency Based Curriculum?

Kenya's CBC, developed by the Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development (KICD), shifts the focus from rote memorisation to the development of practical, transferable competencies. Learners are assessed on what they can do, not just what they can recall.

At EagleNest, our teachers are fully trained and certified in CBC pedagogy. We implement the curriculum through project-based learning, collaborative activities, formative assessment, and rich creative experiences — both inside and outside the classroom.

The 7 Core Competencies

Communication & Collaboration Critical Thinking & Problem Solving Imagination & Creativity Citizenship Digital Literacy Learning to Learn Self-Efficacy

Formative Assessment

Continuous, low-stakes assessment throughout the term — no single high-stakes exam defines a learner.

Learner-Centred Pedagogy

Teachers facilitate discovery; learners are active participants, not passive recipients.

Values & Life Skills

Patriotism, social cohesion, integrity, and Ubuntu are woven throughout every learning area.

Technology Integration

Digital tools and coding are embedded across subjects, not taught as a stand-alone subject.

Pre-Primary School

Our Pre-Primary programme (PP1 & PP2) is built entirely on play-based learning, as required by the KICD Pre-Primary curriculum. We believe young children learn best through exploration, movement, and imagination.

Each day is structured to develop five core developmental domains: language & literacy, numeracy, psychomotor, creative arts, and social-emotional wellbeing — delivered by qualified, caring educators in a warm, stimulating classroom environment.

Primary School (Grade 1–6)

The CBC Primary pathway spans Lower Primary (Grades 1–3) and Upper Primary (Grades 4–6). Each stage builds on the last, deepening competencies and introducing learners to more complex concepts across all learning areas.

Learners are assessed termly through Learner Portfolio Assessment (LPA) and Summative Assessment (SA), giving a holistic picture of each child's progress. Our termly reports are shared with parents alongside a structured parent-teacher conversation.

Junior Secondary School (Grade 7–9)

Kenya's new Junior Secondary (JSS) replaced the traditional Standard 7–8 and Form 1 structure. At EagleNest, Grade 7–9 learners are taught by specialist subject teachers, giving them deep subject engagement alongside the breadth of CBC.

JSS culminates in the Kenya Junior Secondary Education Assessment (KJSEA) at the end of Grade 9, after which learners transition to Senior Secondary School. EagleNest actively prepares learners for Senior Secondary pathways through strong academic guidance and career exploration.

Beyond the Classroom

Co-Curricular & Academic Support

Strong co-curricular programmes and targeted academic support are not extras at EagleNest — they are central to our CBC delivery.

Remedial & Catch-Up Classes

Learners who need extra support attend structured remedial sessions with a subject specialist. Intervention is identified early through continuous formative assessment, not left until exam time.

Gifted & Talented Enrichment

High-achieving learners are stretched through extension tasks, subject olympiads, national competition preparation, and mentorship with University of Nairobi student volunteers.

Peer Study Clubs

Teacher-supervised study clubs meet three times a week. Collaborative peer learning reinforces CBC competencies and builds the communication and teamwork skills the curriculum demands.

Science & STEM Club

Weekly hands-on experiments, robotics builds, and coding projects. STEM club members represent EagleNest at the regional and national science fairs.

Debate & Reading Programme

Our debate team competes in the Kenya Secondary Schools Debate Championship. The Accelerated Reader programme tracks and celebrates reading milestones from Grade 1 upwards.

Pastoral Care & Counselling

A full-time qualified counsellor supports learners' social and emotional wellbeing — a critical CBC component. Class teachers also receive pastoral care training to spot early warning signs.
