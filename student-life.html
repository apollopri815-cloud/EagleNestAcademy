Student Life

Learning doesn't stop when the bell rings. Discover the vibrant world that makes EagleNest a place students love to be.

More Than a Classroom

A Whole Life, Not Just a Timetable

At EagleNest, the friendships formed on the football pitch, the confidence built on a stage, and the curiosity sparked in a science club are just as important as the grade in a report book. Our CBC curriculum requires learners to develop practical competencies, life skills, and social-emotional resilience — and our rich student life programmes are designed to deliver exactly that.

Every EagleNest learner participates in at least one co-curricular activity per week. With over [REPLACE: 20] clubs, sports, and artistic groups to choose from, there is something for every personality and passion.

Meals & Nutrition

A hot, nutritious lunch is available daily from our school kitchen at an additional fee. Menus are planned by a qualified nutritionist and change weekly to ensure variety and balanced micronutrients.

  • Hot lunch Mon–Fri
  • Supervised mid-morning snack break
  • Allergen-aware menus available
  • Packed lunch option welcome
